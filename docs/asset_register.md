### September 14 — sewer and False Exit pass

- `shared/audio/water_impact.wav`: original deterministic synthesized splash, spray and underwater bubbles; generated by `generate_water_impact.py`, no external recordings.
- `shared/shaders/sewer_water.gdshader`: original animated surface normals, current variation and edge foam.
- `PropKit.brickwork()`: original procedural staggered masonry texture.
- False Exit clue, captions and hints: original game writing; no real case evidence or victim material.


### September 14 — remembered house and celestial route

- `celestial_art.gd`, `nursery_sky.gdshader`: original authored glyph geometry, six countable constellation meshes, and blue night backdrop. The same glyph definitions are reused for the sky, figures and floor.
- Fair lady: original procedural child silhouette, opaque pale dress, simple face and hair; no external likeness or imagery.
- `dyer_courtyard.gd`, `star_nursery.gd`, `metropolitan_station.gd`: original procedural scenery; no claim of historical architectural reconstruction.
- New record, puzzle verse, atlas, station note and hints: original text. Historical grounding and source links are recorded in `puzzle_specs/celestial.md`. No real victim names, portraits or crime imagery are used as puzzle content.


### September 14 — memory bells and departure

- `shared/audio/memory_0.wav`–`memory_3.wav` and `train_departure.wav`: original synthesized bell tones and accelerating whistle/chuff texture from `generate_finale.py`.
- House memory sequence, locomotive gauge equations, repair book and completion writing: original game design and text. No real train-operation procedure is represented.

### Narrative revision (2026-09-14)
- `shared/audio/narrator/*.wav`: seven English act introductions, synthesized locally using macOS Daniel at 138 words/minute, converted from AIFF to PCM WAV using afconvert. Synthetic narration, not a human actor recording. Text lives in `ui/story_book.gd` (`VOICES`).
- Original text: opening teaser poem, traveller's book and fictional Whitechapel surveillance instructions.
- Code-native visuals: aged title page, police plan and route overlay, held book, nursery bells/dreamcatchers and covered mortuary form.

### Second narrative pass (2026-09-14)
- `shared/audio/narrator/*.wav`: regenerated by `generate_narration.py` from the verse in `ui/story_book.gd`, using macOS **Daniel** at 148 wpm with silence markers, low-pass warmth and reverb. **Same licence caveat as the sung placeholders:** macOS system voices are for personal, non-commercial use, so they must be replaced by a licensed voice or a human narrator before release.
- `shared/audio/whitechapel_rain.wav`, `heartbeat.wav`, `shadow_steps.wav`: original synthesis from `generate_whitechapel.py`. `shadow_whisper.wav` uses the macOS **Whisper** voice (same caveat).
- `ui/title_page.gd`, `ui/parchment_view.gd`, `ui/police_map.gd`, `ui/story_book.gd`: procedural paper, leather and stone textures, rollers, rules and seals; fonts are system Palatino and Apple Chancery via `SystemFont` (not redistributed; fallbacks apply on Windows).
- `thames_scenery.gd`, Dyer house haunting, Whitechapel grid: original procedural primitives. The Globe, keep and cathedral are loose evocations, not reconstructions.

### Title menu and bridge attack (2026-09-14)
- `shared/menu/london_*.jpg`: eight 1280×720 stills rendered from the game's own scenes by an internal capture script; no external imagery.
- `shared/audio/bridge_crack.wav`, `lady_lunge.wav`: original deterministic synthesis from `generate_bridge_horror.py` (stone groan and cracks; detuned shriek, thump and rushing air). No recordings or voices.

### Walled-in arch, fire brew, co-op (2026-09-14)
- Bricked arch, chain, cross and shoe; bellows and hearth fire; companion traveller avatar: original procedural geometry.
- Witch scraps and revised incantation: original verse, apart from the public-domain Macbeth couplet already listed.
- Networking uses Godot's built-in ENet high-level multiplayer (part of the engine; no additional library).

### Neural narrator replacement (2026-09-14)
- `shared/audio/narrator/*.wav` now uses **Kokoro v1.0 / bm_george** (British English), rendered locally with `kokoro-onnx`. This supersedes the earlier Daniel/macOS provenance for these seven narrator recordings. Model: [hexgrad/Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), Apache 2.0; inference library: [kokoro-onnx](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx), MIT. Synthetic speech, no voice clone or human performance.
- Restrained reading pace, longer closing-question pauses, clean dry speech and consistent level. `narration_cues.gd` is generated from the actual audio boundaries. See `docs/narration.md` for regeneration and the original-audio backup.
